Before you start

What this does: you write down how you work once, and from then on your AI reads it at the start of every chat, on your laptop or your phone.

A Claude accountA ChatGPT accountYou need memory, skills, and connectors. These usually need a paid plan, so check yours under Settings.You need memory, Projects, and the Google Drive app. These usually need a paid plan, so check yours under Settings.
ObsidianFree, from obsidian.md. It's just a notes app for a folder of text files.
A Google accountAny free Gmail works. Drive is how your phone reaches the notes.
About 45 minutesOn Windowsa Mac. Switch computers with the toggle anytime.

Four words you'll see

VaultObsidian's name for a folder of notes. Your 2nd Brain is one folder full of plain text .md files that any app can open.
SkillA saved set of instructions Claude loads by name. Yours will be called /brain.
Project instructionsInstructions that apply to every chat inside one ChatGPT Project. Yours will live in a Project called 2nd Brain.
ConnectorA link that lets Claude read and write files in another service. You'll use the Google Drive one.
App (connector)A link that lets ChatGPT search and read files in another service, and sometimes edit them. You'll use Google Drive.
Mirror vs StreamGoogle Drive's two modes. Mirror keeps a real copy on your computer, which Obsidian needs. Stream leaves files in the cloud until you open them.

Phase 1, about 20 minutes

Build the vault

  1. 01Install Obsidian and make your vault

    Download Obsidian from obsidian.md and open it. The easiest start: unzip the vault template into your Documents folder, then in Obsidian choose Open folder as vault and pick the 2nd Brain folder.

    Starting from scratch instead? Choose Create new vault, name it 2nd Brain, and save it in Documents: C:\Users\your-name\Documents\2nd Brain~/Documents/2nd Brain.

    Don't put it in Downloads. Windows can clean that folder out automatically.It's easy to delete by accident when you tidy up.

  2. 02Check the folders

    If you used the template, these already exist. If not, right-click the left sidebar in Obsidian and choose New folder for each:

    00 Core 01 Areas 02 People 02 Companies 03 Projects 05 Log 06 Archive _interview

  3. 03Let ClaudeChatGPT interview you

    Copy the prompt below, open a new chat, paste it, and answer one question at a time. Paste the interview questions file too if you want it to go deeper. Plan on about 20 minutes; talking with voice dictation is faster than typing.

    Paste into ClaudePaste into ChatGPT Open Claude Open ChatGPT 
    Interview me so you can work the way I do. I'm building a "2nd Brain": a folder of notes you'll read at the start of every chat.

Rules: ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. If an answer is vague, ask one follow-up. Keep it to about 25 questions. If I paste a question list, use it.

Cover:
1. What I'm working on right now, ranked by priority
2. A real day when two things collided, and what I did first
3. Rules I always or never break
4. How I want you to talk to me (length, tone, how hard to push back)
5. The people who matter in my work and what each one is to me
6. The tools I use and what each one is for
7. What a good week and a wasted week look like

When we're done, write four markdown files, each in its own code block with the file name on top:
- Operating Manual.md: my priorities and decision rules, in my own words
- Identity.md: who you are to me and how you should talk to me
- Tool Roles.md: what each tool is for
- interview-[today's date].md: the full questions and answers, word for word

  4. 04Save the four files

    In Obsidian, right-click 00 Core, choose New note, name it Operating Manual, and paste in what the AI wrote. Do the same for Identity and Tool Roles (the template already has empty versions you can paste over). The transcript goes in _interview.

  5. 05Seed a few notes

    Make one short note for each main part of your life in 01 Areas (a class, a job, a club), one for each key person in 02 People, and one per organization in 02 Companies. Two lines each is plenty. Your AI fills them in over time.

You're done when 00 Core has three notes with your real answers in them, and your transcript is in _interview.
Phase 2, about 5 minutes

Teach ClaudeChatGPT to use it

  1. 06Turn on memory

    In Claude, open Settings, find the Memory section, and turn on the setting that generates memory from your chats.

  2. 07Add the /brain skill

    Go to Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills, and upload brain-skill.zip from your starter kit. No editing needed: it reads your 00 Core notes, so it already knows you.

    Don't see Skills? Turn on code execution and file creation on the same page first.

  3. 08Tell Claude to use it every chat

    In Settings, open your Profile and paste this into the personal preferences box:

    Apply /brain at the start of every chat. Keep a dated daily log in the vault.

  4. 06Turn on memory

    In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Personalization, and turn on Memory (saved memories and chat history).

  5. 07Make a 2nd Brain Project

    In the sidebar, choose New project and name it 2nd Brain. Open the project's settings, find Instructions, and paste in everything below the line from chatgpt-project-instructions.md.

  6. 08Start brain chats inside the Project

    Anything you want your 2nd Brain for, start it from the 2nd Brain Project, on your laptop and in the phone app. Optional: add this to Settings, Personalization, Custom instructions:

    When I say /brain, I mean my 2nd Brain Project and the vault in my Google Drive.
You're done when you type /brain in a new chat and Claude recognizes the skill.a new chat inside the 2nd Brain Project tries to look for your vault. It will say it can't find the vault yet. That's expected; the next two phases fix it.
Phase 3, 1 minute

The problem

  1. 09See why you need the next phase

    Your vault is a folder on your computer. ClaudeChatGPT on your phone or the web can't see it, and Obsidian has no official connector. Try it: on your phone, ask "What's in my Operating Manual?" It can't answer yet.

    The fix is to sync the folder to Google Drive, which your AI can reach from anywhere.

You're done when you've seen your AI say it can't reach the vault. Now you know what the next phase fixes.
Phase 4, about 10 minutes

Sync the vault to Google Drive

  1. 10Install Google Drive for desktop

    Download it from google.com/drive/download, install it, and sign in with your Google account.

  2. 11Set it to Mirror files

    Click the Drive icon in the system tray, at the bottom right by the clock (it may be hidden under the little up arrow). Click the gear, then Preferences, then Google Drive, and choose Mirror files.

    Click the Drive icon in the menu bar at the top right of your screen. Click the gear, then Preferences, then Google Drive, and choose Mirror files.

  3. 12Add your vault folder

    Still in Preferences, open My ComputerMy Mac, click Add folder, pick your 2nd Brain folder, and choose Sync with Google Drive. Click Done, then Save.

  4. 13Check the folder on your computer

    Open File Explorer and go to Documents. Your 2nd Brain folder should show a small Google Drive icon.

    Open Finder and go to Documents. Your 2nd Brain folder may show a small sync badge. If you don't see one, step 14 is the real check.

  5. 14Check it on drive.google.com

    Open drive.google.com, click Computers in the left menu, then your computer. 2nd Brain should be there with all its folders.

  6. 15Make sure Obsidian opens the right folder

    In Obsidian, click your vault name at the bottom left, then Manage vaults. Look at the path under each vault. Open the folder that has 00 Core directly inside it, not the folder above it. Remove any extra entries with Remove from list (this doesn't delete files).

  7. 16Only if your vault is in DownloadsWindows

    Open Settings, then System, then Storage, then Storage Sense, and set the Downloads cleanup to Never. Better: move the vault to Documents and redo step 15.

You're done when you see the Drive icon on your 2nd Brain folderyou see 2nd Brain under Computers on drive.google.com, and Obsidian lists exactly one 2nd Brain vault.
Phase 5, about 10 minutes

Connect ClaudeChatGPT and test

  1. 17Connect Google Drive

    In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, find Google Drive, and click Connect. Sign in with the same Google account as step 10.

    In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Apps (some accounts call it Connectors), find Google Drive, and click Connect. Sign in with the same Google account as step 10.

  2. 18Read test

    Inside your 2nd Brain Project, In a new chat, ask:

    Find my 2nd Brain folder in Google Drive and list what's in it.

  3. 19Write test

    Create a note called Sync Test.md in my 2nd Brain folder that says "hello from my AI".

    Wait a minute, then check Obsidian. The note should appear on its own.

    If ChatGPT says it can't create that file on your plan, that's fine. Ask it for the note text instead and paste it into a new note yourself. From then on, end brain chats with "Give me today's log entry" and paste it into 05 Log. Your project instructions already ask for this.

  4. 20Round-trip test

    Add a line to Sync Test in Obsidian, wait a minute for Drive to sync, then ask your AI to read the note back. If it sees your new line, the loop works both ways.

  5. 21Point /brain at Drive

    Update my /brain skill to use my Google Drive 2nd Brain folder by default. Add its folder ID, and keep the safety rules: plain markdown, edit in place, one log file per date.

    When Claude shows the updated skill card, click to save it.

  6. 21Add the folder link to your Project

    Open your 2nd Brain folder on drive.google.com, copy the link from the address bar, and paste it on the last line of your Project instructions where it says "Vault folder link".

  7. 22The real test, from your phone

    Open the ClaudeChatGPT app on your phone, start a new chat inside the 2nd Brain Project, and ask:

    What did I do today?
You're done when your phone answers "What did I do today?" from your vault. Delete the Sync Test note whenever you like.

If something goes wrong

The three things most likely to trip you up, and how to fix each one. Menu names also change as these apps update; if one doesn't match, look for the closest option or ask your AI where that setting lives today.

Two "2nd Brain" vaults show up in Obsidian

You probably opened the folder above your vault, or a copy of it. In Manage vaults, check the path under each one. Keep the one that has 00 Core directly inside it and click Remove from list on the rest. Removing doesn't delete any files.

Obsidian still opens the old location

If you moved the folder after creating the vault, Obsidian remembers the old path. Choose Open folder as vault, pick the folder in its new location, then remove the old entry from Manage vaults. If you'd already added the old folder to Google Drive, re-add the new location in Drive's Preferences too.

The test note never shows up

Drive sync is usually paused or still catching up. Click the Drive icon in the system traymenu bar: if it says paused, resume it, and wait until it says it's up to date. Check drive.google.com first. If the note is there, Obsidian shows it within a few seconds; clicking the folder refreshes it.

That's your 2nd Brain.

The more you use it, the better it gets. Keep your notes in your own words and correct your AI when it's wrong; corrections become rules.

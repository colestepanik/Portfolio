# 2nd Brain interview questions

Paste these into Claude or ChatGPT after the interview prompt, or let the AI ask its own. Answer with voice dictation if typing feels slow. Real examples beat general answers.

## What you're working on
1. What are you working on right now? List everything: classes, jobs, clubs, side projects, applications.
2. Rank that list. If you could only keep three, which three?
3. Which of these has a deadline in the next 30 days?
4. What are you trying to get in the next year (a job, a grade, a skill, a number)?

## How you decide
5. Tell me about a real day when two things collided. What did you do first, and why?
6. What do you always say yes to? What do you always say no to?
7. When you procrastinate, what are you usually avoiding?
8. What does a good week look like, in numbers if you can (hours studied, applications sent, workouts)?
9. What does a wasted week look like?
10. Who do you trust to change your mind, and what would they need to show you?

## Your rules
11. What are three rules you follow without thinking about them?
12. What's a mistake you never want to repeat?
13. What should I never do on your behalf without asking first?

## How you want help
14. Short answers or full explanations? When does that change?
15. Should I push back when I think you're wrong? How hard?
16. How do you like to learn something new: examples, quizzes, reading, doing?
17. What do other AI answers do that annoys you?
18. How do you sign off emails, and how formal are you with different people (professors, recruiters, friends)?

## People and places
19. Who are the five to ten people who matter most in your work right now, and what is each one to you?
20. Which organizations, companies, or teams come up a lot?
21. Anyone I should be careful about, or any relationship that needs a lighter touch?

## Tools
22. What apps do you use every week (calendar, email, notes, school portals) and what is each one for?
23. Where do your to-dos actually live today?

## Wrap-up
24. What do you want this 2nd Brain to do for you in six months?
25. Anything I didn't ask that I should know?

## After the interview
Ask the AI to write four files, each in its own code block: `Operating Manual.md`, `Identity.md`, `Tool Roles.md` (all three go in `00 Core`) and `interview-YYYY-MM-DD.md` (goes in `_interview`).
